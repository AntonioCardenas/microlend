# LendingChain — Product Truth

## Purpose
A decentralized, zero-interest micro-lending platform built on Solana Devnet to celebrate the UN International Day of Charity (September 5). It connects conscientious global lenders with grassroots entrepreneurs in emerging economies—specifically in clean energy, sustainable agriculture, indigenous women-led crafts, and tech education.

## Audience
- **Lenders:** Socially conscious individuals, tech philanthropists, and web3 participants who seek tangible, verifiable human impact with zero intermediary leakage.
- **Borrowers:** Micro-entrepreneurs seeking non-predatory working capital ($500–$4,000) with 0% interest and transparent milestone repayments.

## Value Proposition
Unlike traditional charitable donations where money disappears into administrative overhead, and unlike commercial micro-finance institutions that charge 25%–45% APR, LendingChain:
1. Provides 0% interest capital directly to verified non-custodial Solana project escrows.
2. Enables sub-cent transaction fees ($0.0007), making small $5–$25 micro-loans viable.
3. Features the "Generosity Multiplier" (repayments cycle back to the lender to be re-lent indefinitely).
4. Records dedications and proof permanently on-chain using the SPL Memo Program.

## Visitor Modes
- **Discovery / Home (`/`):** Persuade mode. Inspire trust and action without sensationalism. Show real entrepreneurs, clear numbers, and instant participation.
- **Project Detail (`/loan/[id]`):** Read / Operate mode. Deep factual comprehension of the borrower's enterprise, budget breakdown, and milestone schedule.
- **My Portfolio (`/portfolio`):** Operate mode. Clear tracking of deployed capital, active projects, recovered funds ready to re-lend, and verified impact badges.
- **Transparency Ledger (`/explorer`):** Operate / Audit mode. Cryptographic verification of transactions, block confirmations, and memo data on Solana Devnet.
- **How It Works (`/how-it-works`):** Read mode. Factual, educational comparison between traditional charity/microcredit and on-chain escrow lending.
